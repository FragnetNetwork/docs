# Group Pay is now Donations

Group Pay has been replaced by **Donations** in the [Fragnet dashboard](VAR::PANEL_URL). Your players and friends can still chip in toward your servers. Anyone can donate through your public donation page without a Fragnet account, and other Fragnet users can send credit to your username or email. Every donation goes to your wallet, and renewals use your wallet balance first.

Old Group Pay links don't work any more, so set up a public donation page and share its new link. Credit you received through Group Pay is listed under **Old group pay credits** on the **History** tab of **Donations**.

- [Donations](../control-panel/donations/index.md): how donations work, your donation history and paying invoices with donated credit
- [Public donation page](../control-panel/donations/public-page.md): set up and share a page anyone can donate through
- [Send and receive credit](../control-panel/donations/send-and-receive.md): send credit to another Fragnet user, or receive it from one
